FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use it on sensitive or dry skin?

Yes. NOIRICA uses natural mineral ingredients meeting Japan's Food Sanitation Act safety standards, formulated without unnecessary additives. It has been used by those with sensitive and dry skin — though we recommend a patch test before first use.

Is it suitable for children and men?

Yes, it can be used by all ages and genders. We've received feedback from parents using it on their children's sensitive skin. For children, please use under parental supervision.

How often should I use it?

Most users settle into 2–3 times per week. Building it into your daily skincare as a dedicated rebalancing moment makes it easier to feel consistent, lasting results. Adjust based on your skin's needs.

When will I start to notice results?

Many users notice warmth and smoother pores immediately after use. For longer-term changes (reduced dullness, improved circulation), most report effects within 2–4 weeks at 2–3 sessions per week — results vary by skin type and lifestyle.
