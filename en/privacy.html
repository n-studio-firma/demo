Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 12, 2026

NOIRICA ("we," "our," or "the brand") recognizes the protection of our customers' personal information as an important responsibility and handles it appropriately in accordance with the policy below.

1. Information We Collect

Through our inquiry form, we may collect the following information.

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if voluntarily provided)
  • Salon or business name (for corporate entities and sole proprietors)
  • Any other information included in the content of your inquiry

2. Purpose of Use

Collected personal information is used for the following purposes.

  • Responding to and handling inquiries
  • Providing information about our products and services
  • Consultation and support regarding salon partnerships
  • Analysis for the purpose of improving our services

Should we use this information for any purpose other than those listed above, we will obtain the individual's prior consent.

3. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not provide or disclose collected personal information to third parties, except in the following cases.

  • When we have obtained the individual's consent
  • When disclosure is required by law
  • When necessary to protect a person's life, body, or property

4. Outsourced Services (Google LLC)

We use "Google Forms" and "Google Sheets," provided by Google LLC, to operate our inquiry form and manage response data.

Through these services, personal information you enter is transmitted to and stored on Google's servers. For details on how Google handles personal information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Use of Cookies and Analytics Tools

We plan to introduce analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) on this website in the future. These tools may use cookies to collect user behavior information, such as page browsing history and time spent on the site.

The data collected does not identify individuals and is used for the purposes of improving our site and understanding usage patterns. When such tools are introduced, we will update this policy and provide notice accordingly.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings.

6. Security Measures

To prevent unauthorized access, loss, damage, or tampering with collected personal information, we take the following measures.

  • Restricting access to Google Forms and Google Sheets to the minimum necessary
  • Appropriately managing accounts with access privileges
  • Promptly deleting personal information that is no longer needed

7. Requests for Disclosure, Correction, or Deletion

If you wish to request the disclosure, correction, or deletion of your personal information, please contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable period after verifying your identity.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The content of this policy may be changed without prior notice in response to changes in applicable law or our services. Any revised policy will take effect once posted on this page.

9. Contact Us

For inquiries regarding the handling of personal information, please contact us using the details below.

  • Contact: Inquiry Form
